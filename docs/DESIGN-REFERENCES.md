# Robweb interaction references

Research date: 17 November 2026. Source: [Design Spells](https://designspells.com/), a catalogue of interaction details. Thirty entries were opened and reviewed across mobile/desktop, animation, transitions, navigation, loading, buttons, forms, 404s, skeuomorphism and 3D. The table records the interaction idea only: Robweb must not copy source code, artwork, footage, product shapes or exact timing/visual treatment.

## Selection rules

1. Motion must explain origin, state, hierarchy or completion. If it does none of those, remove it.
2. Core content, routes and controls exist before anime.js or WebGL loads. No autoplay video, cursor replacement or scroll hijacking.
3. Delight belongs at low-frequency moments: first reveal, a deliberate press, a real success or a genuine 404. Repetition becomes noise.
4. Physical metaphors come from Robin’s world—drawing lines, timber, rules, jigs, shavings and fitted parts—without pretending a decorative diagram is technical advice.
5. Every hover/gesture behaviour has keyboard, touch and reduced-motion equivalents. Essential actions are never hidden behind hover, long press, 3D or colour alone.

We have the skills and knowledge to carry out all construction disciplines and we also specialise in the following areas:

- Fine masonry works including brickwork and blockwork
- Traditional roof construction or “cut and pitch”
- Complex timber construction in hard and softwoods
- Staircases
- Structural alterations involving the use of steel and concrete
- Restoration work and listed buildings

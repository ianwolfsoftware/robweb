# RC Calculators: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy explains how the RC Calculators app and website app (“RC Calculators”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) collects and uses your personal data, in line with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. The data controller is Robin Clevett, Ansford Internet, Ansford, Vicarage Lane, Capel, Dorking, Surrey, RH5 5LL. Contact: [email address redacted].

## 1. What we collect

- Account details: your name and email address, and your password (stored only as a secure hash, never in readable form).
- Sign in with Google (Android and web versions only): if you choose it, your name, email address and profile photo from your Google account.
- Your content: the calculations you save, the names you give them, jobs, site addresses, notes, and photos you add (including an optional profile photo).
- Subscription status: whether your yearly subscription is active and when it ends. Payment itself is handled by Apple, Google or Stripe (see section 4); we never see your card details.
- Usage information: a simple record of the days you use the app, so we can see how many people use it. It is linked to your account.
- Technical data: basic device and app information needed to run the service.
We do not collect your precise location, contacts or advertising identifiers, and we do not use advertising or tracking software.

## 2. How we use it, and our lawful basis

- To provide your account and save your work: performance of our contract with you.
- To confirm and manage your subscription: performance of our contract with you.
- To send service emails such as password resets: contract and legitimate interests.
- To keep the service secure, fix problems and understand overall usage: legitimate interests.
We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

## 3. Who we share it with

We use these service providers only to run the app:

- Anthropic: if you use the “ask” feature, the question you type is sent to Anthropic’s API to work out which calculator you need. Only your question is sent, with no account, job or customer details, and we do not keep a copy of it.
- Apple, Google and Stripe: they process subscription payments (Apple App Store on iPhone and iPad, Google Play on Android, Stripe on the web). We receive only confirmation of your subscription status.
- OpenStreetMap (Nominatim): address lookups that you type.
- Our email provider: to send service emails.
- YouTube: to show Robin Clevett’s public videos inside the app.
## 4. Subscriptions and payments

RC Calculators requires a yearly subscription. On iPhone and iPad it is bought and billed through your Apple Account; on Android through Google Play; on the web by card through Stripe. You manage or cancel it in your Apple Account, Google Play subscriptions, or your account on the web. See our Terms of Use for the subscription terms.

## 5. Where it is stored and how long we keep it

Your data is stored on our servers. We keep it for as long as your account is active. You can delete individual calculations, jobs and photos at any time inside the app, and you can delete your whole account from the Account screen in the app. You can also email [email address redacted] and we will delete your account and associated data.

## 6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to our use of your data, and to data portability. To use any of these rights, email [email address redacted]. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

## 7. Cookies and local storage

The app uses your device’s local storage only to keep you signed in and remember preferences such as light or dark mode. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## 8. Children

RC Calculators is intended for tradespeople and is not directed at children under 16.

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the “last updated” date above when we do.

## Extracted copy

Thermowood cladding enquiries are offered at special YouTube-subscriber rates. The page says an initial form is used so the team can prepare a price for a potential order.

Available lengths depend on stock and are stated as 2.4 m to 5.4 m in 0.3 m increments. The page describes Thermowood as thermally modified redwood with a consistent golden-brown colour, greater stability, improved insulation properties and compatibility with stains, oils and lacquers. Other species are also available.

The enquiry form asks for required square metres, lead time, UK location and an optional message. It states that delivery is available only in the United Kingdom and that email verification is required before an enquiry is accepted. Personal contact-field labels are not reproduced in this inventory.
